## Session script

### Context prompt

“Imagine you are reviewing a fictional VoltEdge NovaBook S1 launch email before
publication. You have an approved source that says battery life is up to 12 hours.”

### Tasks

1. Locate the current review and explain what the status means.
2. Open the finding for the claim “up to 20 hours of battery life.”
3. Identify the evidence or evidence gap shown for the finding.
4. Apply, edit, or reject the suggested correction and explain why.
5. Re-scan the revision and describe what changed.
6. Submit the revision for approval and explain who owns the final decision.
7. Open the registry and describe what source/version information you would need to
   trust the VoltEdge fact.

Do not coach participants toward an expected answer. Record the exact task wording,
variant, and any deviations in the session log.
